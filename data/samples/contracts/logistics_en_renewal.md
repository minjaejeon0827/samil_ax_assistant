Party A: Masil Commerce Co., Ltd.
Party B: Harbor Fulfillment Inc. (contact ops@harbor-fulfillment.example, mobile 010-3456-7890)

Article 1 (Purpose) Party B shall provide warehousing, picking, packing and returns handling services to Party A.

Article 2 (Term) This Agreement shall commence on October 1, 2025 and expire on September 30, 2026.

Article 3 (Automatic Renewal) This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date.

Article 4 (Fees) Party A shall pay Party B an annual service fee of KRW 1,500,000,000.

Article 5 (Payment) The annual service fee shall be paid in twelve (12) equal monthly installments on the 25th day of each month, within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice.

Article 6 (Early Termination) If Party A terminates this Agreement before the expiration date, Party A shall pay an early termination fee equal to 40% of the remaining annual service fee.
